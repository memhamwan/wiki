--- 
title: MCH
---
MCH is a satellite site, meaning that there is no RF connection to the rest of the network. It is a standalone router in Marion, AR at a ham radio site that has conventional internet. The device tunnels to MemHamWAN's edge router and gets public IPs to support end user devices that need them.

# Setup

- wireguard1.r1.mch is the interface
- 44.34.130.48/28 is the subnet allocated for this site
